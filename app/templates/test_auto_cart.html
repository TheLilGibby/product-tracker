Test Product Instructions

After adding a test product:

  1. The product will be added to your tracking list
  2. If auto-cart is enabled, the system will automatically try to add it to cart based on your criteria
  3. You can also manually test the "Update" and "Add to Cart" buttons
  4. Different scenarios produce different behaviors:
    • Success: The product is available and can be added to cart
    • Out of Stock: The product is unavailable, but may become available later
    • No Price: The product doesn't have a price (some retailers hide prices)
    • Failure: Adding to cart will explicitly fail
    • Timeout: The operation will simulate a timeout

For more detailed information about auto-cart testing, see the Auto-Cart Testing Documentation.
