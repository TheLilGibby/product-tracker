How to set up the Telegram channel
  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and follow the prompts. Copy the bot token.
  2. Create a channel (or open an existing one) and add your bot as an Administrator with permission to post messages.
  3. Find the channel's chat ID:
    • Public channel: use its username, e.g. @my_deals_channel
    • Private channel: forward a post from the channel to @userinfobot — the ID looks like -1001234567890
  4. Add both values to .env: 
    TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=123456789:ABCdef...
TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID=-1001234567890
  5. Restart the app, then use the test form below.

How to start the tunnel

Quick tunnels get a new hostname every time cloudflared restarts. With the app already running:

Newegg Auto-Cart Settings

Configure cookies for automatic cart functionality on Newegg.com

Paste your Newegg cookies here to enable auto-cart functionality. Format should be the cookie string from your browser.
How to get Newegg cookies
  1. Log into your Newegg account in your web browser
  2. Right-click on the page and select "Inspect" or press F12
  3. Go to the "Application" tab in Chrome (or "Storage" in Firefox)
  4. Select "Cookies" on the left, then click on "www.newegg.com"
  5. Copy the value of the following cookies:
    • NID
    • NSC
    • cto_bundle
    • SoldDate
  6. Format them as: NID=value; NSC=value; cto_bundle=value; SoldDate=value

These cookies allow the auto-cart system to add items to your cart as your logged-in account. They will be saved securely and only used for auto-cart operations on products you select.
